FocusWarden Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Overview

FocusWarden ("the app") is a personal focus and app-blocking application for Android (package com.focuswarden), developed by Usama Waqas. This policy explains what information FocusWarden accesses on your device and how it is used. FocusWarden has no user account or login system — there is no central server collecting your data. Everything the app knows about you lives on your own device.

Information We Collect

FocusWarden requests the following to provide its core features:

  • Location (precise and approximate, including background location): used only to detect when you enter or leave a geofenced zone you set up (e.g. the gym, home) so a blocking profile can arm or disarm itself automatically.
  • List of installed apps: used to show you the list of apps you can choose to block.
  • App/window state (via Android's Accessibility Service): used only to detect when a blocked app is opened, so the block screen can appear. FocusWarden does not read the content of your screen or other apps.
  • Notification metadata (optional, if you enable notification muting): processed entirely on your device to mute notifications from blocked apps during a focus session.
  • Device Admin status (optional, if you enable uninstall-prevention): used only to detect that you've activated this feature; no additional data is collected.
  • Purchase status: whether you've bought the optional "Remove Ads" upgrade, handled by Google Play Billing.

How We Use This Information

All of the information above is processed and stored locally on your device. FocusWarden does not operate its own server, and none of it is transmitted to the developer. It is used solely to run the blocking, geofencing, and notification-muting features you configure yourself.

Third-Party Services

FocusWarden uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: shows ads inside the app and may collect device/advertising identifiers for that purpose. If you are in the EEA or UK, a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) is shown before any ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: processes the optional "Remove Ads" in-app purchase. FocusWarden never sees your payment card details.
  • Map tile providers (Esri "World Street Map" and OpenTopoMap): when you pick a location for a geofence on the in-app map, the map's visible coordinates are sent to these providers to retrieve the map images you see. No other personal data is sent.

Data Storage & Retention

All FocusWarden data — your blocking rules, geofences, settings, and purchase status — is stored locally on your device for as long as the app remains installed. There is no account or login, and the app includes no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Because this data is never transmitted to or stored on a server, there is no server-side retention period, no international data transfer by the app itself, and nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf — uninstalling the app immediately and permanently deletes all of it from your device.

Children's Privacy

FocusWarden is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Ads shown through Google AdMob are configured at the strictest content rating the Google Mobile Ads SDK offers (rated "G").

Your Choices

  • Any permission can be revoked at any time from Android Settings; the corresponding feature simply stops working rather than the app crashing.
  • Ad personalization consent can be reviewed or withdrawn anytime via Settings → Privacy options inside the app.
  • Uninstalling FocusWarden removes all locally stored data immediately — since nothing is stored on a server, this is also how you exercise any right to delete your data.
  • To request details about what a specific permission is used for, or any other question about your data, contact us using the email below.
